Professional Overview

Sriramprabhu Rajendran is an innovative Senior Engineering Leader with 20+ years of experience in financial services and technology, specializing in cloud-native architectures, Generative AI, and enterprise-scale systems. Currently leading multiple engineering teams at Capital One, designing and operating a mission critical ecosystem consisting of more than 100 microservices serving millions of customers.

His technical expertise spans Generative AI, Large Language Models, Agentic Coding, RAG, cloud-native application development, event-driven systems, and high-performance data processing. He has played a key role in building and modernizing mission-critical platforms that handle large transaction volumes while maintaining security, scalability, and reliability.

In parallel with his industry career, Sriramprabhu is an active researcher and contributor to the global academic and professional community. He has authored and co-authored multiple peer-reviewed research papers focusing on areas such as artificial intelligence, machine learning, event-driven architecture, and cloud systems. He has peer-reviewed 80+ research papers for IEEE and various international conferences.

He is a frequent speaker at technical conferences and has been featured in media interviews with ValiantCEO and Authority Magazine. He actively serves the research community as a peer reviewer, technical program committee member, and judge for international conferences, university capstone projects, and industry award programs.
